## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Regional Performance Gap
- **Finding:** South region leads with $232K (30.8%) vs. West's $142K (18.9%) — 63% gap
- **Action:** Replicate South's success factors in underperforming regions
- **Potential:** 10-15% revenue increase

### 2. High-Volume Orders Drive Disproportionate Revenue
- **Finding:** Top 25% of orders generate 38.4% of revenue
- **Action:** Develop bulk-order loyalty program and tiered pricing
- **Potential:** 8-12% margin improvement

### 3. Strong Seasonality Requires Proactive Planning
- **Finding:** 23% revenue variance between January ($72K) and May ($58K)
- **Action:** Align inventory and staffing with seasonal demand
- **Potential:** 5-10% cost savings

### 4. Category Cross-Sell Opportunity
- **Finding:** Balanced performance (Electronics 33%, Groceries 35%, Furniture 32%)
- **Action:** Implement category bundles and "Frequently Bought Together"
- **Potential:** 12-18% AOV increase

### 5. Data Quality is Competitive Advantage
- **Finding:** 100% data quality achieved enables reliable automation
- **Action:** Implement point-of-entry validation; establish data stewardship
- **Potential:** 20-30% reduction in analytical rework
